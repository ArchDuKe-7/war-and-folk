# The War & Folk Project

War & Folk is a Hearts of Iron IV mod that brings you the best mix of military marches, folk tunes, patriotic music and much more. With hundreds of immersive, hand-picked tracks, you won't be dissapointed.

Make sure to follow this page if you are interested in the mod's development. I will be posting updates here before they reach the Workshop.

You may fork or use any part of the mod. No need for credit :)
